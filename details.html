Data sources

  • CO2 Emission by Vehicles (Kaggle), the main dataset: 7,385 rated vehicles from the Government of Canada (model years 2014 to 2020), of which the BMW and Mercedes-Benz rows are used here.
  • Fuel consumption ratings (Government of Canada): the original source of the main dataset, and the 2021 to 2026 petrol and diesel ratings and the battery-electric ratings added here. Open Government Licence – Canada.
  • 2024_Cars_Aggregated.csv, the European real-world (OBFCM) fleet file used in the first version of this project.
  • CO₂ Baseline Database (Central Electricity Authority): the CO₂ of India's grid electricity, used for the electric cars.
  • CO₂ emissions per person in India (Global Carbon Budget, via Our World in Data), used in the everyday comparison.
