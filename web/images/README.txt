Optional car photos for the app
===============================

The app draws its own car illustrations. To show real photos instead, put
your own image files in this folder with exactly these names:

  bmw-sedan.jpg        mercedes-sedan.jpg
  bmw-suv.jpg          mercedes-suv.jpg
  bmw-wagon.jpg        mercedes-wagon.jpg
  bmw-sports.jpg       mercedes-sports.jpg

Any file that is missing simply falls back to the illustration.
Use landscape photos (about 800 x 450 px) that you are allowed to use,
for example your own photos or free-licence photos from Unsplash or Pexels.
